About Us

Passion, Care &
Decades of Love

Luminé Kennels was founded on a simple belief: every puppy deserves the best possible start in life. We pour immense care into each litter — from nutrition and socialisation to health screening and matching puppies with their perfect families.

Our breeding dogs live as beloved family members, not kennelled animals. This makes all the difference in producing confident, well-adjusted puppies.

How It Works

From Enquiry
to Forever Home

1

Get in Touch

Fill in our enquiry form or message us on Instagram. Tell us what you're looking for and a bit about your home.

2

Meet the Pups

We'll share photos, videos, and arrange a visit if you're local — or a video call for those further away.

3

Reserve Yours

A deposit secures your chosen puppy. We'll keep you updated with weekly photos as they grow.

4

Go Home Together

At 8 weeks, your puppy goes home fully vaccinated, microchipped, dewormed, and with a puppy pack.
